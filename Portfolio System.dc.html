// 06 Motion

Motion spec

Two easings only: standard cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1) for things that move, and ease for color and opacity. Under prefers-reduced-motion every transition collapses to 1ms, the count-up jumps to its final value, and the sweep, pulse and pointer glow are off.

// 07 Rationale

Homage, kept quiet

The base is Broadsheet: one serif, paper ground, hierarchy by scale and air. A first-time visitor sees a calm editorial portfolio, not a themed one, and nothing about the reading experience depends on recognizing a reference.

The console cues are structural rather than decorative. The elbow appears once, on the hero corner, and folds to a plain bar on phones. Pill caps mark only what you can press. Registry codes (TH-01, PRJ-02, RD-03, OP-04) and zero-padded readouts give metadata an instrument voice using small caps and tabular figures, with no second typeface.

Motion is the last layer: a one-time sensor sweep across each frame, a faint dot field that brightens under the pointer, a slow status pulse. Station adds the warm and cool pairing as an alternate palette. There are no logos, insignia, names or lettering borrowed from any show, and no screen is recreated.
